📌 Important Formula

Electric field due to a point charge:

E = kq / r²

where q is the charge, r is the distance from the charge, and k is Coulomb's constant.

📝 Practice MCQ

Q1. The electric field due to a point charge is proportional to:

A. r
B. 1/r
C. 1/r²
D. r²
✅ Correct Answer: C. 1/r²
📖 Detailed Explanation

The magnitude of the electric field produced by a point charge is given by:

E = kq / r²

Therefore, when the charge remains constant, the electric field is inversely proportional to the square of the distance.

Hence:

E ∝ 1/r²

Therefore, option C is correct.
